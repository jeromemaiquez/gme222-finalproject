##### GmE 222 Final Project

## Spatio-temporal forest-based hindcasting of gridded population count time series in General Santos City

#### Research Question

> Is the observed 2000-2025 population growth observed in General Santos City lower than expected figures following 1975-2000 growth trends?

#### Objectives

- Perform forest-based hindcasting on 2000-2025 population growth per grid cell in General Santos City based on gridded population time-series data for 1975-2000
- Compare hindcasted versus observed total population growth in General Santos City for the period 2000-2025
